## Project Summary

**Trip Nexus** - AI-powered tourism mobile application developed over 18 weeks.

### Core Features:
1. ✅ Role-based Authentication (Tourist/Advisor/Agency)
2. ✅ Trip Management (Create, View, Delete)
3. ✅ AI Landmark Recognition (Mistral AI)
4. ✅ AI Trip Recommendations
5. ✅ Advisor Search & Filtering
6. ✅ Package Management
7. ✅ Weather Forecast
8. ✅ Smart Notifications (Weather & News)
9. ✅ Notification Panel
10. ✅ Profile Management

### Technical Stack:
- **Frontend:** Flutter (Android)
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas
- **AI:** Mistral AI
- **Weather:** OpenWeatherMap, Open-Meteo
- **Storage:** SQLite, SharedPreferences

### Statistics:
- **Screens:** 15+
- **Services:** 7
- **Models:** 7
- **Duration:** 18 weeks
